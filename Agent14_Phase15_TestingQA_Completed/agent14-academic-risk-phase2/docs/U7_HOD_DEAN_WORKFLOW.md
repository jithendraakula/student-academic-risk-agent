# U7 — HOD / Dean Workflow Consistency

HOD and Dean workflows use the same institutional semantics as the Mentor workspace.

HOD:
- department status
- mentor support demand
- mentor section scope
- student review
- readable risk labels
- department risk profile
- department AI analyst last

Dean:
- institution status
- priority queue
- department oversight
- authorized case review
- risk mix/concentration
- institutional AI analyst last

Open case work is a workload metric, not a student count. Student profile links remain authorized staff case-review links.
